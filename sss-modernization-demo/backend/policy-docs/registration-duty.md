# Registration duty overview

This document describes the registration duty as the demo applies it. It is written for the demo only, and no real person's data belongs in the demo.

## What the registration duty is
In the demo, a registrant has three duties. The first is to create an account. The second is to keep the registration profile complete and current. The third is to apply for an exemption through a case if they believe one applies to them. A registrant who qualifies for no exemption has no case to open.

## Creating an account
An account needs an email address, a password, a full name, a Social Security number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX and a date of birth. The password must have at least 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a digit and a special character. The registrant must be at least 18 years old. Only synthetic Social Security numbers may be used in the demo.

## Profile fields and the registration compliance check
The profile also holds a phone number, an address, an annual income in whole dollars and whether the registrant has documented hardship. Income and hardship are optional. The registration compliance check reads three things: age 18 or older, a phone number on file and an address on file. With all three the status is Eligible. If the phone number or the address is missing, the status is Pending Review. Under 18, the status is Ineligible.

## Exemptions a registrant can apply for
The demo has three exemption types. Type A is for registrants who are 65 or older. Type B is for registrants whose annual income is below $20,000. Type C is for registrants with documented hardship, and a caseworker reviews it. Eligibility is worked out again every time the profile is saved, and a registrant can run the check again with Check Eligibility on the profile page.

## Signing in and roles
Two-step verification with an authenticator app is optional. With it on, signing in needs the 6-digit code from the app. Most people hold the citizen role and see only their own data. Case managers work cases. Leadership sees summary figures only and does no case work. Administrators manage access and the access policy. Staff roles are granted by existing staff in Staff Access, never by registering.
