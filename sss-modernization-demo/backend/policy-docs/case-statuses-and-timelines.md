# Case statuses and typical timelines

## The six statuses
A case has one of six statuses. Draft means the case is not yet submitted. Submitted means it is waiting for a case manager. In Review means a case manager is looking at it. Approved means the exemption is granted and final. Denied means it was refused with a reason. Appealed means the applicant has asked for another look.

## How a case moves
Applying for an exemption opens a case as Submitted. A case manager moves it from Submitted to In Review, and then decides it as Approved or Denied. After a denial the applicant can move the case to Appealed, and a case manager moves it back to In Review. Approved has no further moves. No other change of status is allowed.

## Who moves a case
The applicant submits a Draft case and appeals a Denied case. Case managers review and decide. Nobody can decide their own case.

## Typical timelines: how long a decision takes
The demo promises no decision time. A case with no update for more than 5 days is marked overdue for the case manager, and it counts as stuck on the leadership dashboard. Case managers work from the queue of unassigned cases and from the cases assigned to them.

## What the applicant sees
The dashboard shows each application with a next step. Submitted says the case is waiting for a case manager, and In Review says a case manager is reviewing it. Approved says the exemption is granted and nothing more is needed. Denied says to read the reason and that an appeal is possible. Appealed says the appeal is waiting for review. Every status change creates a notification. The demo's data freshness target is that a change can be read within 30 seconds for at least 95% of updates.
