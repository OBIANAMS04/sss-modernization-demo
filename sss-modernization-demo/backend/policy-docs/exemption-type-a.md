# Exemption Type A: senior (65 or older)

Type A is the senior exemption. It depends on one fact, the registrant's age.

## Who qualifies
A registrant qualifies for Type A when they are 65 years old or older. Age is worked out in whole years from the date of birth on the profile, and a birthday counts on the day it falls. A registrant who is 64 does not qualify. A registrant with no valid date of birth does not qualify.

## How eligibility is decided
The system decides Type A automatically, with no case manager involved. Each time the profile is saved, and each time the registrant selects Check Eligibility, the age rule is run again and the result is saved as Eligible or Not Eligible with a short reason. The reason says whether the registrant is 65 or older or under 65. It does not repeat the exact age.

## Proof of age
An approval of a Type A case needs proof of age on file. The registrant attaches it to the case as a document link of the type proof of age. The decision controls check for it at the moment of approval. An approval without proof of age fails the supporting evidence control and opens a compliance review.

## How to apply
A registrant who is Eligible for Type A selects Apply for this exemption on the profile page. This opens one case for Type A. A registrant cannot open a second case for the same exemption, and cannot apply while the result is Not Eligible.

## What happens next
The case starts as Submitted. A case manager moves it to In Review and then decides it as Approved or Denied. An Approved decision is final. A Denied decision comes with a reason and can be appealed.
