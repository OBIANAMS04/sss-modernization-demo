# Appeals

## Who can appeal and when
Only the applicant can appeal, and only while the case is Denied. An Approved case cannot be appealed because approval is final. A case that is still Draft, Submitted or In Review has nothing to appeal yet. The demo sets no deadline for an appeal.

## How to appeal
Open the case from the Cases page. On a Denied case, select Appeal this decision and confirm. Because an appeal has legal effect, the demo asks for confirmation before it is sent. The appeal is made on the same case, and no new case is opened.

## What happens after an appeal
The case status changes to Appealed and the case waits for a case manager. A case manager moves it back to In Review and decides it again as Approved or Denied. Each step shows on the case timeline, and the applicant is notified when the status changes.

## Reading the denial reason
Every denial records a reason. The applicant sees it on the case page, on their dashboard and in a notification. The reason is the place to start when deciding whether to appeal. More evidence can be attached to the case as a document link before the appeal is decided.
