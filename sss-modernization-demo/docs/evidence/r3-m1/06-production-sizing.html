Production sizing for 10,000 concurrent users

docs/PRODUCTION-SIZING.md. Every number is labelled measured, derived or assumed. A derivation to check with a real load test on the target hosting, not a result.

The result (derived)

PartSize
Applicationabout 4.4 cores busy at the peak, run at 50% or less: 8 to 10 one-vCPU processes across at least 2 availability zones
Databaseabout 5,000 statements/s, about 20 busy connections at peaks: a pooler, a 4 vCPU / 16 GB writer (assumed) and a read replica for the heavy dashboards, Multi-AZ
Audit logabout 37 GB a day before indexes: partition by month
First risksign-ins on a deadline day: bcrypt grows linearly (50 sign-ins/s is 14.5 cores for hashing alone)
