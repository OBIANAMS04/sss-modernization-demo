What the live demo does today

sss-demo-frontend.onrender.com · synthetic data only. The first visit after idle takes about 50 seconds while the free server wakes.

  • Secure sign-upPasswords and SSNs stored as bcrypt hashes; 1-hour sessions; masked SSN entry
  • Profile and compliancePhone, address, income and hardship; checks 18+, phone and address
  • Exemption eligibilitySenior (65+), income (under $20,000), hardship (caseworker review), each with a reason
  • Case managementSubmitted → In Review → Approved or Denied, appeals, timeline and internal notes
  • NotificationsApplicants are notified of every status change, with the reason
  • Roles and auditCitizen, case manager and admin; staff actions and eligibility checks are audit-logged
