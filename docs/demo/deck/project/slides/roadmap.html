Status and roadmap

What the demo does, and what comes next

R0 · Walking skeleton

3 of 5 in demo

In the demo: secure sign-up and sign-in, profile with compliance check, two-step verification. Partly built: data pipeline. Waiting: AWS GovCloud approval.

R1 · Core services

11 of 11 in demo

In the demo: exemption eligibility, case management, compliance validation, data freshness, security audit log, accessibility, citizen, case manager, admin and leadership dashboards, access policies (OPA). Next: R2.

R2 · AI and governance

Not started

Policy answers with cited sources, drift and hallucination monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking, bias testing.

R3 · Scale and launch

Not started

Load tests for 10,000 users, responses under 2 s, multi-region failover, training, runbooks, penetration test, accessibility audit.

Colaberry's scope assessment rated current readiness 33 of 100 on its INPACT scale; the full program targets 86.

The build has 46 stories in four releases, all demonstrated on a synthetic demo deployment; none is operating in production. R0, the walking skeleton, has secure registration and sign-in, the profile and compliance check, and two-step verification working in the demo; cloud infrastructure on AWS GovCloud waits on approval. R1 is complete, 11 of 11 in the demo: exemption eligibility, case management, compliance validation, data freshness, the security audit log, accessibility (Section 508), the citizen, case manager, admin and leadership dashboards, and access policies decided by Open Policy Agent. R2 adds AI with governance: policy answers with cited sources, drift and hallucination monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking and bias testing. R3 covers scale and launch.
