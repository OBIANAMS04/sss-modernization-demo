Status and roadmap

What the demo does, and what comes next

R0 · Walking skeleton

2 of 5 in demo

In the demo: secure sign-up and sign-in, profile with compliance check. Partly built: multi-factor sign-in, data pipeline. Waiting: AWS GovCloud approval.

R1 · Core services

6 of 11 in demo

In the demo: exemption eligibility, case management, compliance validation, data freshness, security audit log, accessibility. Next: role dashboards, API governance.

R2 · AI and governance

Not started

Policy answers with cited sources, drift and hallucination monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking, bias testing.

R3 · Scale and launch

Not started

Load tests for 10,000 users, responses under 2 s, multi-region failover, training, runbooks, penetration test, accessibility audit.

Colaberry's scope assessment rated current readiness 33 of 100 on its INPACT scale; the full program targets 86.

The build has 46 stories in four releases, all demonstrated on a synthetic demo deployment; none is operating in production. R0, the walking skeleton, has secure registration and sign-in plus the profile and compliance check working in the demo; multi-factor sign-in has its server side built, and cloud infrastructure on AWS GovCloud waits on approval. R1 has 6 of 11 stories in the demo: exemption eligibility, case management, compliance validation, data freshness, the security audit log and accessibility (Section 508). Next come role-based dashboards and API governance. R2 adds AI with governance: policy answers with cited sources, drift and hallucination monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking and bias testing. R3 covers scale and launch.
