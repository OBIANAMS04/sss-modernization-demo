Colaberry's scope assessment rated current readiness 33 of 100 on its INPACT scale; the full program targets 86.

The build has 46 stories in four releases, all demonstrated on a synthetic demo deployment; none is operating in production. R0, the walking skeleton, has secure registration and sign-in, the profile and compliance check, two-step verification and the data status card working in the demo; the AWS GovCloud infrastructure is written as code and checked, and waits on approval to be applied. R1 is complete, 11 of 11 in the demo: exemption eligibility, case management, compliance validation, data freshness, the security audit log, accessibility (Section 508), the citizen, case manager, admin and leadership dashboards, and access policies decided by Open Policy Agent. R2 is also complete, 11 of 11 in the demo: policy answers from Claude with cited sources, hallucination and drift monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking, fairness testing and observability; the real-model evaluation and fairness runs on the demo deployment passed on October 21. R3 is complete, 17 of 17: load testing and production sizing, surviving a database outage, monitoring from outside, security testing and hardening, staff training, the incident playbook, runbooks and guides, the accessibility conformance report, and a readiness review with live rollback and restore drills. All 46 stories are addressed. A production launch remains a no-go until ten criteria are met, each with an owner.
