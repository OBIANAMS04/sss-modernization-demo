Colaberry's scope assessment rated current readiness 33 of 100 on its INPACT scale; the full program targets 86.

The build has 46 stories in four releases. R0, the walking skeleton, has secure registration and sign-in plus the profile and compliance check live; multi-factor sign-in has its server side built, and cloud infrastructure on AWS GovCloud waits on approval. R1 has 3 of 11 stories live: exemption eligibility, case management and compliance validation. Next come data freshness under 30 seconds, full audit logging, Section 508 testing, role-based dashboards and API governance. R2 adds AI with governance: policy answers with cited sources, drift and hallucination monitoring, human-in-the-loop review, override tracking and bias testing. R3 covers scale and launch.
