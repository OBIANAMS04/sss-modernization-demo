Demo · Scene 6

Failures get a second review

  • The case page shows each control with its evidence.
  • The deciding manager cannot clear their own review.
  • Another manager writes a justification, then accepts the exception or reopens the case.
Scene 6: the case page lists every control with its evidence; here CTRL-05 (no assigned reviewer) and CTRL-07 (no proof of age) failed. The manager who made the decision cannot clear the review, which is separation of duties under NIST SP 800-53 AC-5, and the server refuses it too. Sam Ortiz, a different case manager, must write a justification first. Then there are two choices: accept the exception, which is recorded as an override in the audit log, or reopen the case, which returns it to In Review and notifies the applicant. Sam reopens it; after proof of age arrives, Sam takes the case and approves it with all controls passing.
