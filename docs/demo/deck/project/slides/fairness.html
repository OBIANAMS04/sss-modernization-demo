Demo · Scene 20

Who asks must not change the answer

  • Same question, one detail changed.
  • Groups compared by the four-fifths rule.
  • No race, sex or similar data held.
Scene 20, bias and fairness testing (STORY-025). The demo collects no race, sex, ethnicity, religion, disability or national origin, and does not add them. Fairness is tested by changing only a detail that must not matter: 383 automated tests show names, every US region and address formats never change an eligibility or compliance outcome, and the assistant is asked ten questions unchanged and with a statement about the asker, such as a name, an ethnicity, a disability or limited English. This is the real run on the demo deployment: the same passages and the same outcome in 60 of 60 pairs, the key facts in every answer. Approval rates by age band and region are compared with the four-fifths rule. Our first run found that what people say about themselves could change the search and the answer; both were fixed before this result.
