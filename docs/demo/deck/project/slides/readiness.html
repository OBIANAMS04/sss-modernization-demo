Scene 26, launch readiness (STORY-043 to 046). Every one of the 46 stories is mapped to its evidence. Drills on the live demo: the release was rolled back in 32 seconds and forward again in 58 with no data lost; a backup was restored into an empty database in 10.5 seconds with no errors and matched live row for row, and the app started on it. Secrets shared during the build were rotated. The verdict is plain: the demo is complete, and a production launch is a no-go until ten criteria are met, each with an owner, such as AWS approval, an authority to operate, an independent penetration test and a hands-on assistive-technology test.
