Demo · Scenes 1 and 2

Sign up and check eligibility

Registration: masked SSN entry and strong passwords. The SSN is hashed before it is stored.

Eligibility: every exemption type returns a status and a plain-language reason. Apply in one click.

Scene 1: a registrant creates an account. The banner reminds everyone this is synthetic data. The SSN field is masked with a Show toggle, passwords need 12 characters with a capital, a digit and a symbol, and the SSN is stored only as a one-way hash. Scene 2: the profile page checks the record (18 or older, phone, address) and shows what is missing. Each exemption type returns Eligible, Pending Review or Not Eligible with a reason. Jordan Rivera qualifies for Type A because the applicant is 65 or older. The exemption types in the demo are illustrative rules, not Selective Service policy.
