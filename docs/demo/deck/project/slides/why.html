Why it matters

Registration status follows people for years

Ages 18 to 25

Federal law requires nearly every man living in the United States to register in these years.

Years later

Registration status can affect eligibility for federal jobs and some state programs.

Every request

Registrants, agencies and staff all need fast, accurate answers from complete, current records.

The Selective Service System keeps the national registry that federal law requires nearly every man living in the United States to join between 18 and 25. Registration status matters long after: it can affect eligibility for federal jobs and some state programs. That is why registrants, other agencies and SSS staff all depend on records that are complete, current and easy to check.
