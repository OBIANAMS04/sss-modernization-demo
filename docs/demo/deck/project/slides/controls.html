Decision controls

What every decision is checked against

ControlWhat is checkedBasis
CTRL-01The approval matches a current eligibility resultProgram eligibility rules
CTRL-02Eligibility re-checked after the last profile changePrivacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a(e)(5)
CTRL-03A denial records its reason and notifies the applicantAPA, 5 U.S.C. 555(e)
CTRL-04The person deciding is not the applicantNIST SP 800-53 AC-5
CTRL-05The case is assigned to the manager who decides itProgram policy
CTRL-06The decision is made from In ReviewProgram policy
CTRL-07The required document is on fileProgram policy
CTRL-08The decision is in the audit log: who, what, whenNIST SP 800-53 AU-3
These are the 8 controls checked automatically on every decision. CTRL-01 and CTRL-07 apply to approvals; CTRL-03 applies to denials; the rest apply to every decision. CTRL-02 reflects the Privacy Act's requirement for accurate, timely records used in determinations. CTRL-03 reflects the Administrative Procedure Act's requirement to state the grounds for a denial. CTRL-04 is separation of duties (NIST SP 800-53 AC-5) and CTRL-08 is audit record content (AU-3). The required documents are proof of age for Type A, an income statement for Type B and hardship evidence for Type C.
