Demo · Scene 17

Policy answers that cite their source

  • Answers come only from the policy.
  • Every statement opens its quote.
  • Each answer is checked before it shows.
Scene 17, the policy assistant (STORY-017 to 020 and 022). A registrant asks a question in plain words. The answer comes from Claude, but only from the demo's policy documents: eight synthetic documents, clearly labelled as not Selective Service policy, split into 43 passages. Every statement carries a numbered citation that opens the exact quoted text. Before anyone sees the answer, a check confirms that every sentence sits under a citation and every number appears in a cited passage; an answer that fails is withheld. Social Security numbers are removed from a question before anything is searched or stored. On the demo deployment, the 33-question evaluation against Claude scored 100% on retrieval, facts, citation validity and grounding.
