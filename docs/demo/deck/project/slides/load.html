Tested under load

  • Twice the throughput after fixes.
  • No failures up to 128 connections.
  • Sized for 10,000 users.
Scene 22, load and performance (STORY-030 to 033). A pinned load harness ran four journeys, registrant, case manager, policy assistant with a stand-in model, and dashboards, from 4 to 128 open connections on a local copy, before and after the fixes. Before, a full database connection pool was mistaken for an outage and 96 to 98% of requests failed at 64 and 128 connections; after the fixes none failed at any step, throughput about doubled and the 95th percentile at 16 connections halved. One light run against the live demo, 25 connections for a minute, kept p95 at 1.24 seconds with no errors. Production sizing for 10,000 concurrent users is derived from these measurements, with every figure labelled measured, derived or assumed; nothing here was run at 10,000 users.
