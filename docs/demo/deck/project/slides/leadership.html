Leaders see the program, not the people

  • KPIs, 12-week trends and bottlenecks.
  • Aggregates only: no applicant data.
  • Administrators see the whole operation.
Scene 15, the leadership and admin dashboards (STORY-014 and 015). A director sees total applications, approval rate, average processing time, open cases and appeal rate; applications and decisions per week; where cases wait; and team performance. No applicant's name or email reaches this page or its API, and leadership does no case work. Administrators have their own dashboard with all cases, team performance and system health, and give leadership, case-manager and administrator access in Staff Access.
