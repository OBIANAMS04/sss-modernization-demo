Demo · Scene 18

People decide, not the assistant

  • Personal questions go to a case manager.
  • Staff approve, correct or decline.
  • Overrides are tracked against 20%.
Scene 18, human oversight (STORY-023 and 024). The assistant never decides a person's eligibility or case. A question about the asker's own case, an answer that fails the citation check, and any answer a person reports all go to the Assistant Reviews queue. A case manager, never the person who asked, approves the draft, corrects it or declines, with a note for the record; the person is notified and sees the reviewed answer. The override rate counts how often staff went against what the system said, across corrected answers, accepted compliance exceptions and decisions against the eligibility check, with a 12-week trend against a 20% target on the admin and leadership dashboards.
