Walk the seven problems. Five are solved in the live demo today. Registrants can see where they stand: accounts, eligibility results with a plain-language reason, a case timeline and notifications. Decisions carry reasons: a denial cannot be saved without one, and applicants can appeal. Every decision is checked against 8 controls, and failures go to a second case manager. SSNs are stored only as one-way hashes, never shown back or logged, and every route declares who may use it. Case work happens in one queue. Two are planned: data freshness under 30 seconds and Section 508 testing are the next R1 stories, and FedRAMP hosting on AWS GovCloud waits on approval, with multi-factor sign-in screens to follow.
