Walk the eight problems. Seven work in the demo today, on a synthetic deployment rather than in production. Registrants can see where they stand: one dashboard with each application, its status and the next step, eligibility results with a plain-language reason, recent activity and notifications. Decisions carry reasons: a denial cannot be saved without one, and applicants can appeal. Every decision is checked against 8 controls, and failures go to a second case manager. SSNs are stored only as one-way hashes, never shown back or logged, every route declares who may use it, and every read, export and refusal is recorded in a write-once audit log. Case work happens in one queue. Every change is timed until the next request can read it, against a 30-second target. Every screen is checked against WCAG 2.1 AA, which includes Section 508, for keyboard, screen readers, contrast and reflow. One is planned: FedRAMP hosting on AWS GovCloud waits on approval, with multi-factor sign-in screens to follow.
