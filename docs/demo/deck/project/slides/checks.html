Scene 5: when a case manager approves or denies a case, 8 controls run at once: eligibility basis, a fresh eligibility check, reason and notice for denials, separation of duties, case ownership, review before decision, the required document, and an audit entry. In this scenario, Dana Whitfield approved Jordan Rivera's case while it was unassigned and without proof of age. That decision failed two controls, so 2 of 3 decisions passed, a rate of 66.67% against the 99% target, and the dashboard raises an alert with the open review listed underneath.
