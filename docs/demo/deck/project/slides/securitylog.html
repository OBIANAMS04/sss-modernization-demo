Demo · Scene 10

Every action is on the record

  • Sign-ins, refusals, reads, exports and changes, with who, when and from where.
  • The database refuses edits and deletions; entries are kept seven years.
  • No SSN, password or secret is ever written to the log.
Scene 10, security audit logging (STORY-010): every security-relevant event is recorded with who, what, when, the result and the caller's address. That covers registrations, sign-ins including failed ones (filed under the address that was tried, never the password), sign-outs, MFA steps, every refusal, every successful read of the API, exports, profile changes (field names only), decisions and overrides, role grants, deployments, configuration changes and alerts. Here a case manager searches for one applicant and sees the whole trail, newest first: two staff pages refused with 403, the application and its document, the eligibility check, a profile change that lists the fields but not the values, the sign-in, and two refused sign-ins, one before the account existed and one with a mistyped password. The database refuses any edit, deletion or truncation of the log, even by its owner, and each entry carries a retain-until date seven years on. A changed setting such as the sign-in lifetime is logged with its old and new value; a secret is named, never its value. Demonstrated on a synthetic demo deployment; production would also copy every entry to separate append-only storage on AWS.
