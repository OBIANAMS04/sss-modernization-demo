What the app is for

One place to register, apply, decide and prove it

Registrants

Register, keep their record current, see which exemptions they qualify for and why, apply and appeal.

Case managers

Work one queue, take ownership, decide with a stated reason and export case lists.

Oversight

See whether decisions follow the rules, review the ones that do not, and search every result.

The platform modernizes the business systems behind registration: online registration and registrant records, profile updates with compliance checks, exemption and appeal case management, compliance verification and reporting. Three groups use it. Registrants manage their own record and applications. Case managers work and decide cases. Compliance staff and leadership check that every decision followed the rules.
